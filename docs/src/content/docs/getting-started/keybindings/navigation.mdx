## `↑/k` - Move Up

Press <kbd>↑</kbd> or <kbd>k</kbd> to move to the previous work item in the current section.

## `↓/j` - Move Down

Press <kbd>↓</kbd> or <kbd>j</kbd> to move to the next work item in the current section.

## `←/h` - Previous Section

Press <kbd>←</kbd> or <kbd>h</kbd> to move to the previous section in the current view.

## `→/l` - Next Section

Press <kbd>→</kbd> or <kbd>l</kbd> to move to the next section in the current view.

## `g/home` - First Item

Press <kbd>g</kbd> or <kbd>Home</kbd> to move to the first work item in the current section.

## `G/end` - Last Item

Press <kbd>G</kbd> or <kbd>End</kbd> to move to the last work item in the current section.

## Mouse

Unless you set [`mouse`](/ghtui/configuration/defaults/#mouse-mouse) to `false`, you can use the mouse:

- Scroll over the list to move to the previous or next work item.
- Scroll over the preview to scroll it.
- Scroll over the section tabs to move to the previous or next section.
- Click a work item to select it.
- Click a section tab, or the arrows beside the tabs, to switch sections. Click the new section button to add a section.
- Click a view in the footer to switch to it, and click `? help` to show help.
- Click a tab in a PR preview to show it.
- Click the search bar to search, and click a suggestion to insert it.
- Click outside the help popup to close it.
